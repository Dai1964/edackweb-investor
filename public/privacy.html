Privacy Notice

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This notice explains what happens to your personal data when you use the "Register Your Interest" form on this page. It applies only to this enquiry form — EdackWeb's other products (the website builder demos, LOCIFY Build) are separate systems with their own data handling.

Who's responsible for your data

EdackWeb (Dai Evans)
Contact for any privacy question or request: edack.david@gmail.com

EdackWeb is currently a small, early-stage operation. This notice will be updated with fuller company details if/when EdackWeb's legal structure changes.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form:

  • Your name
  • Your location (town/city)
  • Your current occupation/background
  • Whatever you choose to write in "why this interests you"
  • Your email address
  • The date/time you submitted the form

Please don't include anything sensitive in the free-text field (health information, for example) — we don't need it and don't ask for it.

Why we collect it, and our lawful basis

We use this information to respond to your enquiry and have a conversation about whether an EdackWeb territory partnership is right for you. Our lawful basis under UK GDPR is Article 6(1)(b) — processing necessary to take steps, at your request, before potentially entering into an agreement with you.

Who sees it

Your enquiry is stored on our hosting provider (Railway) and a notification email is sent to us via Resend, a US-based email delivery service. That transfer is covered by the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (the "UK–US Data Bridge"), which the UK government recognises as providing an adequate level of protection. We don't sell, rent, or share your details with anyone else, and we don't use them for marketing.

This site also loads a web font from Google Fonts, which means your device's IP address is visible to Google at the moment the page loads — the same as almost any website using a hosted font service.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for up to 12 months from the date you submit it, or until you ask us to delete it sooner, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • See what we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your data
  • Stop using it

Email edack.david@gmail.com and we'll action it within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes to this notice

If how we handle your data changes, we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
